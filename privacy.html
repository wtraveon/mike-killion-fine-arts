YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy Policy.

Last updated: November 17, 2026

About this policy

This policy explains how MIKE KILLION Fine Arts handles information you choose to provide when you use this website. The website presents artwork and provides ways to contact the artist; it does not offer online checkout or account registration.

Information you provide

If you use the inquiry form, the information you submit may include your name, email address, and message. Please do not include payment card details, passwords, or other sensitive personal information in a message. If you contact the artist by phone or email, the information you share in that communication is also used to respond to you.

How inquiries are used

Information submitted through the inquiry form is used to receive, review, and respond to your question about artwork, availability, or a commission. The form sends the inquiry to the artist at navmike8@gmail.com. Your email address may be used as the reply-to address so the artist can respond. Your email address may be used as the reply-to address so the artist can respond.

Service providers

The inquiry form is processed by a Cloudflare Pages Function and uses Resend to deliver the message by email. Those providers process information as needed to operate their services. Their handling of information is also subject to their own terms and privacy practices.

Retention and security

Messages delivered by email may remain in the recipient's email account and backups until they are deleted under the account's retention practices. This website does not provide a customer account or an online order-history system. Reasonable technical measures are used to protect the website and its inquiry endpoint, but no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Cookies, analytics, and external links

This website does not intentionally require an account or a cookie-based login to browse the artwork. It loads typography from Google Fonts. Links to external websites and services are governed by the privacy practices of those sites. This policy does not make claims about data those third parties may collect when you visit their services.

Your choices and questions

You can choose not to use the inquiry form and contact the artist directly. To ask about an inquiry you submitted or request that a message be deleted from the artist's email account where practicable, contact navmike8@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the website or its services change. The date at the top of this page indicates when it was most recently revised.
