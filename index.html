A LIFE IN ART

Meet Mike.

“I assess myself as a semi-semi-realist painter and muralist, seeking to capture as many actual details as I can so you can place yourself into the environment I am portraying.”

Mike Killion has been drawn to art since childhood. A painting of a Canadian goose in flight, brought home by his older brother, sparked an interest that grew through school, college, exhibitions, commissions, and a career in mural work.

His subjects range from tropical fish and golf courses to serene landscapes, architecture, wildlife, athletes, and people in action. He also paints his dreams as a memorial to what Yahweh is teaching him at the moment.
